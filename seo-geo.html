Local SEO · Google Maps · Nail salon, Montréal

Getting a Montréal nail salon found on Google Maps

A busy salon wanted to appear in Google Maps and “near me” searches in English and French. I built the local keyword plan and quotation, and optimized the Google Business Profile and its linked social profiles.

  • 20+ local and “near me” keywords with a top 5 goal, led by “nail salon” (about 12K searches a month)
  • Google Business Profile at 4.9 stars with 1,350+ reviews
  • Facebook and Instagram profiles linked to show in Google results
SEO · U.S. print-on-demand eCommerce (Shopify)

From about 5K to about 430K organic visits in one year

A U.S. print-on-demand store on Shopify, with products reachable through many collection URLs. I audited every canonical tag and set one primary collection per product, so each product has one main URL.

  • Organic traffic grew steadily from about 5K to about 430K visits a month (May 2023 to May 2024)
  • One canonical URL and one primary collection per product
  • About 1,700 organic pages and 640 referring domains by May 2024
SEO · U.S. nail supplies eCommerce

Recovering organic traffic after a 78% drop

A U.S. nail-supply store with thousands of SKUs lost most of its Google clicks to 17,000+ junk URLs, thin product pages and broken internal links. I cleaned up the index, rewrote product content, rebuilt internal links and added FAQ schema for AI search.

  • Daily Google clicks back up from about 170 to a peak of 450, after falling from about 780
  • 17,000+ unindexed URLs sorted into four groups: keep, canonical, noindex, or redirect and remove
  • Product pages rewritten with uses, specs and how-to steps; links tied Product → Brand → Category
  • FAQ schema added to prepare for Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT
AIO · Furniture eCommerce

From almost never cited to cited in over half of AI test questions

A furniture site ranked top 3 to 5 on Google but appeared in only 1 of 22 AI test questions. In 4 weeks I added schema markup, answer-first content, real author profiles and brand mentions.

  • Google AI Overview: 0 to 14 of 22 questions
  • ChatGPT: 0 to 12 of 22 questions; Perplexity: 1 to 9 of 22
  • AI referral traffic: from about 50 to about 1,000 sessions a month
GEO · Exhibition contractor, Vietnam

Recommended by ChatGPT for exhibition booth construction

I built three websites for an exhibition contractor and widened its offer from booth construction for exhibitions and fairs to showrooms and theme parks. I led link building, content direction, internal links, technical audits and UX frameworks, working with developers on user-friendly layouts. Now ChatGPT lists the client as a recommended contractor.

  • Listed #2 of 10 contractors in ChatGPT for the exhibition-booth query
  • Cited with its website and portfolio link, plus rating and review count
  • Three new websites and a wider product and service portfolio
  • Briefed content writers on link building, anchor text, canonicals and on-page SEO, and managed the budget
