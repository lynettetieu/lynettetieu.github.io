Marketing strategy · Local business · Nail salon, Tennessee (U.S.)

Nearly doubling revenue for a local nail salon

A salon had hit its revenue ceiling. I rebuilt the service menu around premium services, launched a monthly seasonal pedicure, and ran local ads within 5–10 miles. Keeping repeat customers while adding new ones made each new customer stack on top of the last.

  • Revenue up about 96%, from about $210K to about $411K in 4 months
  • Menu rebuilt around Gel-X, deluxe pedicures and head spa combos to lift average order value
  • A new seasonal pedicure each month, and free upgrades instead of discounts
  • Targeted ads for higher-income customers within 5–10 miles

Scaling lead generation across 125 Meta campaigns

Lead-form, Messenger and remarketing campaigns for property projects, tested and refined from Dec 2024 to Oct 2026.

  • About CAD 167K in total spend across 125 campaigns (Dec 2024 – Oct 2026)

  • 9,024 leads in total
  • Cost per lead of about CAD 3.8 on the best-performing campaigns
