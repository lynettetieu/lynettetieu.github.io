Campaign - Healthcare

HPV awareness campaign for women

As an Account Executive, I coordinated and executed an HPV awareness campaign for a global healthcare company in Vietnam, educating women about reproductive health risks and preventive care. I supported social media content planning, creative coordination and campaign execution.

  • 58 Facebook posts across 3 campaign phases
  • About 14M impressions and 22.4K engagements
  • About 14.5K clicks from ages 35–44 and 13.5K from ages 45–54

Campaign · Finance and banking

“One Touch”: full-scale campaign for a digital banking launch

Led communication strategy and execution for a bank’s OneBank “One Touch” campaign, from the key message to social video.

  • Single, memorable message built around “touch”
  • Open Banking video: 24K views, 123 reactions, 106 comments, 71 shares (as shown)
  • About 1M total app downloads during the 2022–2025 campaign, moving users from the old app to a new version with many convenient functions
  • About 420K net-new installs
  • Cost per install of about CAD 4.3 (₫80K)
