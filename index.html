About

From events to digital: how I got hooked on marketing.

Where it started. I graduated with a bachelor’s degree in event and leisure management, then interned at an agency on events and brand activations to gain hands-on experience. The agency let me try different roles, and that is when my passion for marketing began.

How I work. Events are just one part of promotion in the marketing 4Ps, and they were my way in to understanding other promotion channels and real-world marketing. Instead of following ready-made frameworks and templates, I put my full effort into customer insights and behaviour, plus each company’s long-term goals, to build strategies that are effective and feasible. I keep adapting in real time to the latest changes in search engines and AI platforms.

“From business goals to conquering the market.”
